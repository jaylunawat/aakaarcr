Frequently Asked Questions

[ ALL YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT BEING A COLLEGE REPRESENTATIVE ]

What is an Aakaar College Representative (CR)? +

A College Representative is an official ambassador of Aakaar, IIT Bombay within their own university or institute. They lead the student delegation, disseminate technical updates, publicize competitions, and facilitate campus participation.

What are the eligibility requirements? +

Any registered college student enrolled in undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma programs in Engineering, Architecture, Construction, or Allied Sciences is eligible to register. Passion for networking and technical leadership is all you need!

What is the duration of the CR tenure? +

The program commences immediately upon registration confirmation and concludes with the grand offline fest of Aakaar 2026 at the IIT Bombay campus in March.

Is there any registration fee to apply as a CR? +

No. Applying and working as a College Representative for Aakaar, IIT Bombay is 100% free of charge.
