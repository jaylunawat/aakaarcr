01 What is an Aakaar College Representative (CR)?
+

An Aakaar College Representative (CR) acts as the central bridge between IIT Bombay's Department of Civil Engineering and your undergraduate/polytechnic college. You represent Asia's largest civil engineering festival on your campus.

terminal
Primary Duties: Publicize events, coordinate student contingent registrations, organize local pre-fest workshops, and serve as the single point of contact for festival authorities.
02 What are the eligibility requirements?
+

Any undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma student actively enrolled in a recognized university or polytechnic institution across India is eligible. While students in Civil Engineering, Architecture, and Allied Construction disciplines are preferred, dynamic students from other branches with leadership drive are also welcome.

  • Must hold a valid College Identity Card.
  • Must commit approximately 2-4 hours per week for outreach and tasks.
  • Good interpersonal and network communication skills.
03 What is the duration of the CR tenure?
+

The official tenure operates for 5 Months, typically starting October 2025 and culminating in the grand finale of Aakaar at the IIT Bombay campus in March 2026.

Milestone checkpoints are conducted every fortnight where your progress points are logged into your portal ledger.

04 Is there any registration fee to apply as a CR?
+

NO. ZERO FEES. ABSOLUTELY FREE.

Aakaar IIT Bombay does not charge any application or registration fee to join the College Representative Program. Selection is purely based on applicant motivation, college reach, and submission responses. Beware of third parties claiming otherwise.

05 How are CR points and leaderboard rankings calculated?
+

Points are credited via our automated portal based on verifiable proof uploads for assigned quests:

Social Media Outreach: +50 to +150 EXP per post
Competitions Reg Referral: +250 EXP per team
Offline Noticeboard Display: +100 EXP per photo
Webinar / Info Session: +500 EXP per event

The public Leaderboard recalculates rankings every 24 hours at 00:00 IST.

06 When do we receive the IIT Bombay certificate and merchandise?
+

Digital verifiable certificates signed by the Convener and Faculty Advisor of the Civil Engineering Association, IIT Bombay are dispatched within 14 working days post-fest.

Physical loot boxes (T-shirts, branded stationery, tech badges, and hardcopy certificates) are shipped directly to your mailing address or handed over personally if you attend the fest on campus.

07 Do CRs get free entry and accommodation at IIT Bombay during Aakaar?
+

Yes, for qualifying top-tier performers! The top-ranking CRs on the leaderboard earn sponsored on-campus hostel accommodation, free festival entry passes, and VIP workshop seat reservations during the fest weekend in Powai, Mumbai.

hotel Tier 1 CRs receive complimentary 3-day stay inside IIT Bombay campus.
08 Can multiple students apply from the same college?
+

Yes. Depending on the size of your campus, we induct between 1 to 3 Representatives per institution. If multiple students apply, candidates may either collaborate as a joint collegiate task force or be selected based on their initial screening profile.
